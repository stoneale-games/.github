# Code of Conduct

Stoneale is a mental wellness project, so we hold our own community to the same standard of care we want for our players.

## Our pledge

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as our Code of Conduct. Everyone participating in Stoneale spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and community chats) agrees to follow it.

In short, we pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and first-time contributors
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Respect people's privacy. Never share anyone's personal or health information
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Mocking or trivializing mental health conditions
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Spam, including low-effort pull requests opened only to farm rewards

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the contact details at [stoneale.com](https://stoneale.com). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close pull requests, or ban contributors who violate this Code of Conduct, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
